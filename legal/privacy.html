Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

SalonBook is an online booking service for salons, available at {{SITE}}.{{OPERATED_BY}} This policy explains what information SalonBook collects, what it is used for, and the choices you have. It covers two groups of people: clients, who book appointments, and salon owners, who have an account.

Information we collect

If you book an appointment

  • Your name and phone number, and your email address and any notes if you choose to give them.
  • The service, date and time you booked, and the deposit amount.
  • The MMG transaction ID you enter, or the payment result MMG sends back if you pay through MMG's checkout page.

SalonBook never sees or stores your MMG PIN, password, or account balance. Deposits are paid directly to the salon through MMG.

If you are a salon owner

  • Your name, email address, and a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password. We cannot read your password.
  • Your salon's details: name, phone, address, opening hours, services, prices, logo, and MMG payment details, including MMG merchant keys if you turn on MMG checkout.
  • If you connect Google Calendar, the information described under Google user data below.

Collected automatically

  • One sign-in cookie for salon owners, which keeps you signed in for up to 14 days. SalonBook uses no advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across other sites.
  • Your IP address, held briefly in memory to limit repeated attempts (for example, too many wrong passwords). It is not saved to our database.
  • Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

How we use information

  • To take, hold, confirm, and cancel bookings, and to show each salon its own appointments.
  • To email salon owners when a client books or pays. These emails include the client's name, contact details, and booking details.
  • If a client gives an email address, to send them emails about that booking only: a confirmation, a reminder before the appointment, and a notice if the salon cancels. Salons can switch these off.
  • To add confirmed bookings to a salon owner's Google Calendar, if they have connected one.
  • To sign owners in, send password-reset links, and protect the service from abuse.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

  • The salon you book with. Its owners see your booking and contact details. Other salons on SalonBook cannot.
  • Google Calendar. If a salon owner has connected their calendar, a confirmed booking is saved there as an event containing the client's name, phone number, email (if given), service, deposit, notes, and booking code. That event lives in the owner's own Google account.
  • MMG. If you pay through MMG checkout, the amount and a payment reference are sent to MMG to process the payment.
  • Service providers that run the service for us: our hosting and network providers, and the email provider that delivers notification emails.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Google user data

Salon owners can choose to connect a Google account so that confirmed bookings appear in their Google Calendar. Connecting is optional, and clients are never asked to sign in with Google.

What we access

  • Your Google account's email address, so the Settings page can show which account is connected.
  • Permission to add and remove events on your calendar (the calendar.events permission).

How we use it

  • When a booking is confirmed, SalonBook creates one event for it on your primary calendar.
  • When that booking is cancelled, SalonBook deletes the event it created.

That is all SalonBook does with your calendar. It does not read, list, change, or delete any of your other events, and it does not access your contacts, email, or files.

How we store and protect it

We store your Google email address and the access key Google issues for your calendar (a "refresh token") in our database, on a server that only the operator can access. The access key is stored encrypted. Connections to SalonBook and to Google are encrypted with HTTPS.

Sharing

We do not sell Google user data, share it with anyone, use it for advertising, or use it to develop or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. No person reads it, except where needed for security, to comply with the law, or with your permission.

SalonBook's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting

  • In SalonBook, go to Settings and disconnect Google Calendar. Your Google email address and access key are deleted from our database straight away.
  • You can also remove SalonBook's access at any time from your Google account, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Events already added to your calendar stay there until you delete them, because they are in your own Google account.

How long we keep information

Bookings and account details are kept for as long as the salon uses SalonBook, so that the salon has a record of its appointments. You can ask for your information to be deleted sooner; see below.

Your choices

  • Clients: ask the salon you booked with, or contact us, to see, correct, or delete your booking details.
  • Salon owners: you can change your salon's details and turn notification emails on or off in Settings. Contact us to close your account and delete your salon's data.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed, the site is served over HTTPS, each salon can see only its own data, and sign-in and booking forms are rate-limited. No online service can promise perfect security, but we work to protect your information.

Children

SalonBook accounts are for salon owners and are not meant for children. A parent or guardian should make bookings on behalf of a child.
