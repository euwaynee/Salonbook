What SalonBook is

SalonBook gives each salon a booking page. Clients pick a service and time and pay a deposit through MMG; the salon confirms the booking. SalonBook is a booking tool only. The salon provides the service, and the appointment is an arrangement between the client and the salon.

Bookings and deposits

  • A time slot is held for a limited period while the deposit is paid. If no payment is reported in time, the slot is released.
  • A booking is confirmed only once the salon, or MMG's checkout, confirms that the deposit was received.
  • Deposits are paid directly to the salon through MMG. SalonBook does not receive, hold, or refund money. Refunds, cancellations, and rescheduling are decided by the salon, so contact the salon about them.
  • Give accurate details when you book, so the salon can reach you.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, SalonBook adds confirmed bookings to your calendar and removes them if they are cancelled, as described in the privacy policy. Calendar sync is a convenience: if Google is unavailable, a booking can still be confirmed, so treat your SalonBook dashboard as the record of your appointments.
